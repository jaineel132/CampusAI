Normalization Case Study - Aarav Shah (CS301)

Table: Library(BookID, Title, AuthorID, AuthorName, MemberID, MemberName, IssueDate)

Functional dependencies:
BookID -> Title, AuthorID
AuthorID -> AuthorName
MemberID -> MemberName
(BookID, MemberID) -> IssueDate

1NF: all attributes are atomic, so the table is already in 1NF.
2NF: Title and AuthorID depend only on BookID, and MemberName only on MemberID (partial dependencies on the key (BookID, MemberID)). Split into Book(BookID, Title, AuthorID), Member(MemberID, MemberName), Issue(BookID, MemberID, IssueDate).
3NF: AuthorName depends on AuthorID, which depends on BookID (transitive). Move it to Author(AuthorID, AuthorName).
Final schema: Book, Author, Member, Issue. Every non-key attribute now depends on the key, the whole key, and nothing but the key.
